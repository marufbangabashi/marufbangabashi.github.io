Md. Maruf Bangabashi

Lecturer, Department of Computer Science and Engineering
Dhaka International University, Bangladesh

I work on computer vision and medical image analysis, with a focus on making deep-learning results trustworthy. My M.Sc. research at Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University audits popular image benchmarks for near-duplicate leakage, showing how naïve data splits inflate reported accuracy and how models rely on shortcuts such as colour rather than disease features. I am also interested in self-supervised learning, test-time adaptation, multimodal fusion, and action recognition.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 and would be glad to hear from faculty working on related problems.
